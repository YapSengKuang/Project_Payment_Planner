# Customer validation guide

## Research goal

Determine whether Australian freelancers or small agencies have a repeated need to plan deposits and milestone receipts against project expenses, and a concrete reason to switch from current tools. This is a hypothesis, not a validated market claim.

## Recruitment

Recruit 5 to 8 owners who quote fixed-price client projects and decide payment terms. Include owners who recently experienced a shortage and owners who did not. Use the owner's own recruitment channels; obtain permission before contacting anyone. No outreach has been sent by this project.

Suggested invitation: “I am researching how owners plan project costs and client payments. Would you be willing to discuss a recent fixed-price project for 25 minutes? This is research, and you do not need to share client identities or confidential financial details.”

Ask permission to take notes. Use participant IDs and anonymised amounts. Keep contact details and raw sensitive records outside this repository.

## Interview script for 25 minutes

1. What kind of fixed-price projects do you deliver, and who decides the payment terms?
2. Walk through your most recent project from quote to final receipt. What costs occurred and when?
3. How did you choose the deposit and milestones? When did invoices go out and when did money arrive?
4. Show an anonymised spreadsheet or describe your current method. What took effort or went wrong?
5. Describe the last time you paid a project expense before receiving enough client money. What did you do, and what did it cost you?
6. What have you tried to solve that problem? What would make you change your current approach?
7. Are project amounts GST inclusive or exclusive? How do weekends, late receipts and same-day expenses affect your planning?
8. After discussing their current behaviour, show the proposed journey. Ask them to explain a funding gap in their own words. What would they change before using it for a real quote?
9. May we follow up for a pilot with a sanitised example?

Avoid pitching features before understanding actual behaviour. Do not treat hypothetical willingness to use or pay as proof of demand.

## Compare current alternatives

For each tool actually used by participants, record its name, dated source or observed workflow, cost when relevant, schedule planning support, gap visibility, scenarios, export, and specific missing capability. Include spreadsheets and manual processes. No competitor comparison has yet been performed; verify product claims against current primary sources when doing it.

## Sprint 0 review gate

Review 5 to 8 completed interviews. Choose one segment and country using evidence. Summarise the repeated problem, current alternatives and concrete reason to switch, including contradictory findings. Collect 3 to 5 anonymised real reference projects and independently check calculations. The synthetic fixtures in this repository are preparation only.

Approve or revise scope, tax convention, same-day ordering, date and version rules. Set pilot measures for time to plan, completion, gap understanding and changed payment terms. If evidence is weak, revise the hypothesis before implementation; record the decision and its evidence.
